CS foundations: mechanisms & connections

From data organization and hardware execution to system management and network communication, the focus is on causal links across layers.

Data structures

Data structures and algorithms

  • Use cases for lists, trees, graphs, and search structures
  • Sorting stability, complexity, and storage behavior
  • KMP, graph traversal, and representative recursive algorithms
  • Deriving algorithmic cost from data organization
Computer organization

Computer organization

  • Data representation, arithmetic circuits, and flags
  • Instruction sets, datapaths, and control units
  • Cache, virtual memory, and main-memory organization
  • Pipelines, interrupts, DMA, and bus timing
Operating systems

Operating systems

  • Process states, scheduling, synchronization, and deadlocks
  • Page tables, TLBs, resident sets, and page replacement
  • File systems, disks, and I/O management
  • Mapping abstractions to hardware execution
Networks

Computer networks

  • Encapsulation and forwarding from link to application layer
  • IPv4 / IPv6, routing, and address resolution
  • TCP reliability, flow control, and congestion control
  • DNS, HTTP, and common application protocols
Mathematics

Conditions, derivation & geometric intuition

Formulas are placed back into their conditions and geometric objects, then tested through derivation, region analysis, and error review.

Calculus

Calculus

Limits, derivatives, integrals, series, and multivariable calculus, with emphasis on regions, substitutions, line and surface integrals, and spatial geometry.

Linear algebra

Linear algebra

Matrix operations, linear systems, eigenvalues, and quadratic forms, with attention to rank conditions and geometric meaning.

Probability

Probability and statistics

Distributions, joint densities, independence, moments, and parameter estimation, with probability expressed through regions and integrals.

Optimization

Optimization

Connecting gradients, convexity, duality, and proximal operators to image restoration as a foundation for Primal–Dual and Plug-and-Play methods.

Image restoration

Models, priors & experimental evidence

Starting from degradation, this map connects optimization priors, deep models, evaluation metrics, and engineering constraints.

Image foundations
Convolution, frequency domain, point-spread functions, sampling, noise models, and PSNR / SSIM.
Optimization priors
Total variation, proximal mappings, variable splitting, ADMM, and Primal–Dual.
Deep models
CNNs, residual networks, UNet, Transformers, and training stability.
Experimental methods
Degradation consistency, dataset splits, metric interpretation, memory optimization, and reproducible records.
