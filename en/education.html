Learning method

Learning method & practice

Starting with computer science and mathematics, I use paper reproduction and experiment debugging to turn concepts into methods that can be explained, implemented, and tested.

Capability path

How the pieces come together

Build a foundation, let projects expose details, then return to first principles to explain results and correct the implementation.

Foundation

Computer science and mathematics

Systematic study of data structures, computer organization, operating systems, and networks, supported by calculus, linear algebra, and probability.

Research

Image restoration and deep learning

Starting from convolution, frequency analysis, and regularization, then extending to CNNs, Transformers, Plug-and-Play, and Primal–Dual methods.

Practice

Paper reproduction and experimental validation

Translating degradation models, loss functions, and inference steps from papers into code, then checking them with metrics, visual results, and targeted comparisons.

Engineering

Maintainable implementation

Managing dependencies, data, logs, checkpoints, GPU memory, and documentation so experiments can be rerun, compared, and extended.

Method loop

From understanding to verification

Each step leaves an inspectable derivation, implementation, or result instead of only a memorized conclusion.

01

Define conceptual boundaries

Identify the object, conditions, and conclusion; separate similar concepts; and connect prerequisites to later use.

02

Preserve the decisive steps

Record the steps that make a formula or algorithm valid, then test each condition with a representative problem.

03

Expose details through code

Use implementation to test boundary conditions, data formats, numerical ranges, and computational cost.

04

Trace results back to causes

When metrics fail or NaNs, color shifts, or OOM errors appear, inspect the input, model, optimization, and inference chain in order.
