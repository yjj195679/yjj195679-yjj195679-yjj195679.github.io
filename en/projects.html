Audio intelligence

VocalPureNet encoding and denoising

A multi-objective study of audio quality, storage size, and computational cost.

Time-frequency pipeline

Combined STFT, energy, zero-crossing rate, spectral centroid, entropy, and crest factor with a two-stream U-Net for noise classification and vocal denoising.

Measured outcome

The adaptive scheme reduced overall file size by 25.49% and distortion by 70.78%; the report recorded roughly 95% accuracy across 14 noise classes.

Engineering reliability

Thermoelastic optimization for chip packaging

BGA and QFN corner reliability under thermal mismatch and solder-ball defects.

Composite modeling

Used mixture rules, laminate theory, Voigt and Schapery models to estimate effective Young’s modulus and thermal expansion.

FEM result

The defective BGA model produced 3.82×10⁻² mm corner displacement, 22.86 ppm/°C effective CTE, and 27.75 GPa effective modulus.

Combinatorial optimization

Municipal waste collection routing

Routing and scheduling under capacity, time-window, transfer-station, and asymmetric-road constraints.

Hybrid heuristics

Combined Clarke–Wright savings, K-means, nearest-neighbor construction, simulated annealing, and greedy fleet assignment.

Operational result

A nine-vehicle plan met the 480-minute limit, followed by transfer-station selection and ten-year cost and daily carbon estimates.

Cooperative optimization

UAV smoke-screen deployment

Three-dimensional motion and visibility geometry coupled with high-dimensional heuristic search.

Model and search

Used point-to-segment distance and discrete time steps with differential evolution, genetic algorithms, and simulated annealing.

From one device to a fleet

The optimized single-device plan achieved 4.9 s of effective screening; three coordinated UAVs reached 16.1 s.

Distributed analytics

MovieLens user preference analysis

An end-to-end Hadoop, HDFS, Hive, and HiveQL workflow on a three-node cluster.

Data pipeline

Designed HDFS directories and Hive tables, then cleaned, joined, and aggregated a one-million-rating sample from MovieLens 32M.

Findings and limits

Popular films had more consistent ratings, broader-viewing users rated more strictly, and the 3% sample remained a stated limitation.

Machine learning

Titanic survival prediction

A controlled comparison of C4.5, random forest, and logistic regression.

Evaluation

Compared accuracy, precision, confusion matrices, and ROC–AUC on the same train/test split.

Result

Random forest performed best with 83.41% test accuracy and 0.889 AUC, ahead of C4.5 and logistic regression.

Java development

Campus lost-and-found system

Functions

Registration, login, password recovery, guest access, role-based permissions, and CRUD operations for lost and found records.

Robustness

Full-stack · Supabase

Bilingual guestbook and secure admin console

A lightweight backend for public messages, administrator replies, and content moderation on this website.

Postgres and RLS

A messages table stores names, posts, timestamps, visibility, and replies. Row-level policies limit anonymous users to public reads and constrained submissions.

Auth, MFA, and abuse controls

Administrative access requires an Auth account, the admin app-metadata role, and TOTP at AAL2. Input checks, a honeypot, and a database trigger add rate-limit protection.

Multivariate statistics

Applied multivariate analysis

Inference, classification, dimension reduction, and composite evaluation on socioeconomic and business data.

Methods

Hotelling’s T², Wilks’ Lambda, hierarchical clustering, K-means, discriminant analysis, PCA, and factor analysis.

Interpretation

Compared standardized and raw-data classifications to show how scale can alter distance structures and cluster boundaries.
