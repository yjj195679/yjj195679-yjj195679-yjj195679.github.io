Audio intelligence

VocalPureNet encoding and denoising

Balancing quality, size, and computation.

Method

Time-frequency features and a two-stream U-Net for classification and denoising.

Result

File size fell 25.49%, distortion fell 70.78%, and 14-class accuracy reached about 95%.

Engineering reliability

Thermoelastic optimization for chip packaging

Thermal mismatch and defects in BGA and QFN packaging.

Method

Equivalent material models, finite-element analysis, and sensitivity checks.

Result

Corner displacement 3.82×10⁻² mm, effective CTE 22.86 ppm/°C, and modulus 27.75 GPa.

Combinatorial optimization

Municipal waste collection routing

Balancing cost and emissions under practical constraints.

Method

Savings, clustering, nearest-neighbor construction, and simulated annealing.

Result

A nine-vehicle plan with station, ten-year cost, and daily carbon estimates.

Cooperative optimization

UAV smoke-screen deployment

Three-dimensional motion, visibility geometry, and coordinated search.

Method

Geometric screening tests with differential evolution, genetic algorithms, and simulated annealing.

Result

One device achieved 4.9 s of screening; three coordinated UAVs reached 16.1 s.

Distributed analytics

MovieLens user preference analysis

Million-rating analysis on a three-node Hadoop cluster.

Pipeline

HDFS and Hive for cleaning, joining, and aggregation.

Findings and limits

Popular films had steadier ratings; the 3% sample limits broader inference.

Machine learning

Titanic survival prediction

A controlled comparison of three classifiers.

Evaluation

Accuracy, confusion matrices, and ROC–AUC on one train/test split.

Result

Random forest performed best with 83.41% accuracy and 0.889 AUC.
