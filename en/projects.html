01 · Super-resolution

DPSR reproduction

Variable splitting separates data consistency from the super-resolution prior, allowing one model to handle different blur kernels.

Implementation

Training and degradation modeling

Trained SRResNet on DIV2K with online Gaussian, motion, and disk kernels, convolution, and bicubic downsampling. The model uses 16 residual blocks, L1 loss, and Adam.

DIV2KSRResNetL1 LossAdam
Inference

PnP-ADMM inference

Variable splitting alternates data-consistency, super-resolution-prior, and dual updates, allowing the model to accept explicit blur kernels and handle varied degradations.

Engineering

8 GB memory adaptation

Added patch-wise inference and tiled reconstruction to avoid full-image memory overflow, with consistent normalization, kernel handling, and device selection.

Result

Reproducible experiment pipeline

Completed training, checkpointing, test-image inference, and comparisons. L1 loss decreased from about 0.089 to 0.033.

02 · Optimization

Primal–Dual deblurring

The objective combines data fidelity with total variation and is solved iteratively with the Chambolle–Pock framework.

Model
Minimizes reconstruction error against the blurred observation while TV regularization preserves edges and suppresses noise.
Dual update
Applies ascent and projection to the gradient variable, constraining the dual variable to the feasible set defined by regularization.
Primal update
Uses FFT under periodic boundaries to compute a closed-form linear update and avoid pixel-wise solving.
Experiment focus
Step-size conditions, kernel alignment, boundary artifacts, noise level, and regularization balance.

03 · Deep restoration

UNet / Restormer

A comparison of convolutional networks, Transformers, and optimization-based post-processing centered on stability, diagnosis, and verified fixes.

Stability

NaN and color-shift diagnosis

Standardized tensor ranges, channel order, and loss inputs to fix divergence and color shifts caused by inconsistent normalization.

Memory

Memory and resolution trade-offs

Used cropping, batch-size adjustment, gradient-free validation, and patch-wise inference to retain practical resolution within about 8 GB of GPU memory.

Architecture

Restormer training

Trained a Transformer restoration model on paired sharp and blurred images, saving the best weights and tracking validation results.

Hybrid

Optimization-based post-processing

Combined deep-network outputs with Primal–Dual constraints to examine sharpness, noise, color stability, and computational cost.
